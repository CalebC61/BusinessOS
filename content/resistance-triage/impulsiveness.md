# Impulsiveness

**Signal:** distraction language — the user started or intended to start, but something else pulled focus.

**Routed protocol:** environment-design prescription (remove the specific distraction trigger identified by the user) plus an offer of `11-commitment-devices.md` (pre-commitment stakes or temptation bundling) — opt-in, not defaulted on.

**Delivery:** Coaching chat (AGENTS.md Role 3) asks what specifically pulled focus before prescribing an environment change — a generic "turn off notifications" prescription without identifying the actual trigger is a card-authoring failure, not an acceptable response.
