## Purpose

Unlike the other cards, this one isn't delivered to the user — it's a constraint on how every other card and every UI surface is written. Autonomy-supportive language throughout, competence made visible via the VACR trend (not a single pass/fail number), no shame mechanics anywhere in copy, and relatedness supported by the honest Sunday Review artifact the user can't get anywhere else.

## Script (personalization slots)

Applies as a review checklist to every other card's copy, not a rendered script:
- Does the language invite a choice (autonomy) rather than issue a command with no rationale?
- Does the feedback show trend/progress (competence) rather than a binary win/lose?
- Is there zero shame, guilt, or streak-loss framing anywhere in the string?

## Contraindications

Never gamify with loss-aversion mechanics (e.g., "your streak is about to die!") — that's a competence/shame lever the theory explicitly warns against for sustainable intrinsic motivation, and it's excluded on the same grounds "willpower training" is excluded (PRD §6.3).

## Evidence reference

Deci, E. L., & Ryan, R. M. — Self-Determination Theory; intrinsic motivation requires autonomy, competence, and relatedness.
