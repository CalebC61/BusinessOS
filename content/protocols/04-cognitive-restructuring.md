## Purpose

Routed from the Resistance Triage `low_expectancy` branch (`content/resistance-triage/low-expectancy.md`) when a skip is driven by prediction language ("it won't work," "I'll fail"). Runs a structured thought record on the specific failure prediction before it's accepted as fact, rather than letting the prediction silently cancel the action.

## Script (personalization slots)

> **Situation:** `{{situation}}`
> **Automatic thought:** `{{prediction}}`
> **Evidence for:** what actually supports this?
> **Evidence against:** what have you seen that argues against it?
> **Balanced thought:** given both sides, what's a more accurate prediction?
> **Confidence rating:** how sure are you of `{{prediction}}`, 0–100%?

The confidence rating is the pre-registered number a chained Behavioral Experiment (`05-behavioral-experiments.md`) will later check against what actually happens.

## Contraindications

This is not a debate to "win" the user out of their thought — a balanced thought that's still realistically negative (e.g., a genuinely underpriced offer) should stand. The card's job is accuracy, not forced positivity.
